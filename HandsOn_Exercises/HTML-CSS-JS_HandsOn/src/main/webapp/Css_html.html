Community Newspaper Bulletin

The City Council is pleased to announce new infrastructure projects starting this autumn. These developments aim to improve public transit, extend cycling paths, and revitalize our historic downtown district. Citizens are invited to public forums held every Thursday evening at City Hall to provide feedback and ask questions directly to city planners.

Additionally, public library services are expanding with digital literacy programs, after-school tutoring sessions, and weekend coding bootcamps. Registration for all council-sponsored events remains completely free for residents. Make sure to update your user profile online to receive monthly neighborhood newsletters and urgent council alerts.
